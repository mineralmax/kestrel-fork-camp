Two creeks meet here and turn into a swimming river. We built a small camp around that spot… and then mostly got out of the way.

Friday · 4:10 pm

The road gets narrower.

Pavement turns to gravel at the red barn. Your phone drops to one bar, then none. Somebody in the car says “oh,” and nobody answers, because they’re all looking at the same trees.

Friday · 6:30 pm

First fire.

The ring is already stacked with split fir. Light it badly, laugh about it, light it again. By the time dinner’s done, you’ve stopped checking for the time.

Saturday · 7:15 am

Mist on the meadow.

You wake before anyone, boil water, and stand in the doorway holding a cup with both hands. The whole valley is breathing out.

Saturday · 2:00 pm

Get on the river.

In June it runs fast and white, and a guide will put you in a raft. By August it’s a slow green float. Either way, everyone shrieks at the first splash and nobody wants to be first out.

Saturday · 10:40 pm

More stars than you planned for.

We’re a certified dark-sky site, which means the Milky Way is not a metaphor. Lie back on the meadow. Pick a satellite. Lose it.

Sunday · 11:00 am

Nobody wants to leave.

Check-out is at eleven. You’ll do a slow lap of the site, then another. On the way down the gravel, someone says it: “Next month?”

Good to know

Questions we get a lot.

Is there cell service?

Spotty at the lodge, none at most sites. The lodge has fast Wi-Fi, and cabins have a landline for emergencies.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Dogs must be leashed outside your site and never left alone. $15 per stay in cabins, tipis and trailers, free at campsites.

What if it rains?

Then it’s cozy. The lodge has board games, a wood stove and hot cider. Tipis and cabins stay dry. We’ll lend you a tarp for tents.

Are fires allowed?

Fire rings are provided and allowed except during Oregon fire restrictions, usually late summer. We’ll switch you to propane fire tables and tell you before you arrive.

How accessible is the camp?

One cabin and the bathhouse are fully step-free, with accessible parking near the lodge. Tell us what you need and we’ll prepare it.
