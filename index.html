Welcome to Kestrel Fork

A small camp, run slowly, on purpose.

Two creeks meet here and turn into a swimming river. We built the camp around that spot, with twenty-two sites, nothing crowded, and a lot of dark sky.

Every stay comes with a fire ring, a stack of split fir, and access to the bathhouse with real hot showers. Most people arrive Friday and wish they’d booked through Monday.

Good to know

Questions we get a lot.

Is there cell service?

Spotty at the lodge, none at most sites. The lodge has fast Wi-Fi, and cabins have a landline for emergencies.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Dogs must be leashed outside your site and never left alone. $15 per stay in cabins, tipis and trailers, free at campsites.

What if it rains?

Then it’s cozy. The lodge has board games, a wood stove and hot cider. Tipis and cabins stay dry. We’ll lend you a tarp for tents.

Are fires allowed?

Fire rings are provided and allowed except during Oregon fire restrictions, usually late summer. We’ll switch you to propane fire tables and tell you before you arrive.

How accessible is the camp?

One cabin and the bathhouse are fully step-free, with accessible parking near the lodge. Tell us what you need and we’ll prepare it.
